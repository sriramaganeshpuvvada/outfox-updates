1. What Outfox is

Outfox (listed on Google Play as "Outfox: Scam & Fraud Alert", so it is not confused with the Outfox board game) is a free advice tool that looks for warning signs of scams in the messages, links, calls, pictures and app activity on your phone, and tells you what it found. It does not block calls or messages, and it never acts for you.

2. Outfox cannot catch every scam

  • Scam methods change every day. Outfox can miss a scam, and it can warn about a genuine message, link, number or company.
  • A warning means "warning signs were found", not that any person, number, website or company is a criminal or a fraud. Treat it as advice, check for yourself, and contact the organisation through its official number or website.
  • A message without a warning is not proof that it is safe.

3. You stay in control

You decide what to do with every message, call and payment. Never share an OTP, PIN or password, never install an app a caller asks you to install, and never pay someone because a message or caller pressures you.

Outfox works on your phone. It uses the internet only to update scam patterns and when you start something that needs it (revealing a short link, the password leak check, or sending a report), as the Privacy policy explains.
