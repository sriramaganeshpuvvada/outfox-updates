Privacy policy

Version 4 · effective 14 November 2026

The short version

Outfox (on Google Play: "Outfox: Scam & Fraud Alert") checks everything on your phone. Your messages, calls, contacts, photos and browsing are never uploaded, sold or shared. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only personal data that ever reaches us is what you choose to send us by email.

1. What Outfox looks at, and why

  • Message shield (notification access): the text of notifications you receive, to find scam signs the moment they arrive. Checked on the phone and then forgotten.
  • Call shield (caller ID and spam app role): the number of an incoming call, to warn about numbers reported in scams and about hidden or faked numbers. Calls are never blocked, heard or recorded.
  • Your country: read from the country code of your SIM card, else the mobile network, else your phone's region setting (no permission needed), or the country you pick in Settings. It is kept on the phone only and is used to show Scam Academy lessons, the trap game, Scam Radar stories and helplines for your country. It is never sent to us. "Delete my data" clears it.
  • Danger moments (usage access and phone state): which app is open and whether a call is in progress, only to warn you when a screen-sharing or payment app is opened during a call. Not stored.
  • Alert on top (display over other apps): to show the warning above other apps. Nothing is collected.
  • Check: text, links, numbers or pictures you choose to check. A picture is used only to read a QR code or the words in it, on the phone, and is not kept.
  • Call family (phone calls): only when you tap "Call now", to call the person you saved.

Before any shield that reads personal data is turned on, Outfox explains it and asks you to agree. The date you agreed is kept on the phone and shown under More › Privacy.

2. What is kept on your phone

  • Warnings: at most 50, for at most 30 days, each with a short preview (140 characters, long numbers such as OTPs and account numbers hidden).
  • Counters (messages checked, warnings, scams stopped), your settings, the family contact and safe word you entered, your quiz streak, and the dates you agreed to these terms and to each shield.

Delete it at any time: More › Privacy › Delete my data, or uninstall Outfox. Outfox has no account and no copy of this data anywhere else.

3. What leaves your phone

Outfox uses the internet only for the daily pattern update and for things you start yourself: revealing a short link, the password leak check, and reports or emails you choose to send. Nothing else connects.

  • Scam pattern updates: once a day Outfox downloads the latest patterns from the Outfox update service. Nothing about you is sent; like any website, the service sees your internet address.
  • Reveal short links (only if you tap it or turn it on): Outfox asks the link-shortening service (for example bit.ly) where a link leads, without opening the page. That service sees your internet address and that the link was checked.
  • Reading words in a picture (only when you check a picture with no QR code): done on the phone by Google ML Kit in Google Play services. The picture and its words are not sent anywhere unless you add them to a report you send; Play services may download the recognition model once and, under Google's terms, send Google anonymous statistics about how the feature performs.
  • Password leak check (only when you use it): Outfox scrambles the password on your phone (SHA-1) and sends only the first 5 characters of the result to the free Pwned Passwords service (api.pwnedpasswords.com), which answers with a list to compare on the phone. Leak data is provided by Have I Been Pwned (haveibeenpwned.com), credited as its licence asks. The password itself never leaves your phone and is not stored.
  • Copied text: read only when you tap "Check what I copied", and only checked on the phone.
  • Reports and emails you send: your email address, your message, the caller's number or message text if you add them (including words the phone read from a screenshot you chose), and the app version, pattern version, language and Android version that the report adds. Never include OTPs, PINs or passwords; long number runs are hidden when we store a report.

4. Support emails

  • Used only to answer you and to improve scam detection.
  • Read automatically by our support system, which answers common questions and passes the rest to a person within 48 hours.
  • Stored in our support mailbox (Google Gmail) and in a private ticket system with long numbers hidden.
  • Deleted within 180 days. Ask "delete my data" by email at any time and we delete your emails and remove your details from ticket records.
  • Never sold, never used for advertising.

5. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and similar laws, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Write to support.outfox@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. To withdraw consent for a shield, pause it in the app or turn off its permission in your phone's settings.

6. Children

Outfox is meant for adults. People under 18 may use it only with a parent's or guardian's consent and supervision; a parent or guardian should contact support for them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, Outfox shows it again and asks you to agree before you continue.
